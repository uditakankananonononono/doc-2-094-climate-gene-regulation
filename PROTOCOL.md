## Gates

C1 Transcriptomes: actual measured-expression file links with species, tissue, biological units, replicate definitions and collection times documented.
C2 Exposure: measured weather or assigned temperature/irradiance exposure at the sampling hour, including circadian timing, not daily means with documented time/location match to molecular samples. Seed provenance/climate normals alone cannot represent experimental exposure. Controlled treatment is an intervention but chamber temperature profiles are not field climate; any chamber-only result is controlled-environment, never climate-stress-in-nature.
C3 Join keys: explicit sample-to-exposure linkage and spatial/temporal resolution sufficient to avoid guessed sites/dates. Cohort/site matching is named as such, not individual assignment.
C4 Conservation: at least two distinct species with documented compatible gene/pathway or ortholog annotation sources, not two cultivars counted as species. Metadata PASS means annotation sources exist, not conserved programs. Actual feature matching is reserved for outcome protocol. A is ineligible for C4.
C5 Holdouts: at least three independent geographic sampling sites plus at least three distinct species within one study or documented common-protocol design, with >=2 independent biological replicate units per exposure/species/site cell. Two sites or species stays UNRESOLVED/scoped. A provenance biome is not an independently exposed field site. A contributes only C1-C3, C6 and geographic feasibility, never C4. Geographic and species holdouts must both be possible; repeated time samples from one plant cannot fill replication.
C6 Identification/terms: distinguish observational association from assigned intervention; record confounding, batch/circadian/developmental factors, outcome-derived sample corrections and source-use terms. Free public access only, licenses UNKNOWN stay UNKNOWN, raw redistribution not inferred from article/code license.

GO only when C4/C5 and the other gates are verified within one design for geographic/species transfer at a documented unit. STOP when a required design floor is documented impossible in the fixed set. UNRESOLVED when documentation/admission insufficient. A cross-study union does not satisfy C5 unless protocol, tissue, sampling time and exposure measurement are shared; such a union remains UNRESOLVED at best. Expected F0 may be STOP for full 094. Controlled-multispecies B is a separate narrower question. A controlled multispecies heat study without geographic exposures is a scoped alternative, not full 094 completion.
